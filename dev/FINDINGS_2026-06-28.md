# Automated-testing findings — 2026-06-28

First comprehensive run of the autonomous scenario harness against the **real
interactive window** (68 user-story scenarios driving symbols, timeframes, all 19
indicators, all 13 display flags, 21 themes, 9 styles, pane types, watchlist CRUD,
alerts, multi-feature journeys, and adversarial stress). Result after fixes:
**66/68 pass**; the 2 reds are the open findings below.

## Fixed this session

### 1. CRASH — Ichimoku Chikou span underflows usize → whole app dies
`src-tauri/src/chart/renderer/compute.rs:162`. `for i in 0..(n - kijun)` panicked
with *attempt to subtract with overflow* whenever a series had fewer bars than the
kijun period (26) — e.g. a short 1w/4h history. The panic on the compute thread
poisoned shared state and tore down the whole window (caught first as a hard kill
mid-suite). **Fix:** `0..n.saturating_sub(kijun)` (empty Chikou when too few bars).

### 2. Stochastic %D signal line is always NaN (never renders)
`compute.rs` `compute_sma` seeded its rolling sum from `data[..period]`. When used
to smooth Stochastic %K into %D, those first elements are NaN warmup, so the
running sum stayed NaN for the entire series — the %D line never drew. **Fix:**
NaN-aware seeding (skip leading NaN, like `compute_ema` already does). Verified:
STOCH now reports a finite %D; `canvas_indicator_value_in_range` passes 0–100.

### 3. dev-inspector `is_clipped` mislabeled every small icon button
`src-tauri/src/dev_inspector/mod.rs:96` computed `is_clipped = min_side < 28px` —
a touch-target heuristic, not clipping. Every 20–24px icon button was flagged
"clipped", which broke `no_clipped_widgets` / `design_audit_clean` **and** the
human Ctrl+Shift+I design audit (false positives drowned out real issues).
**Fix:** true geometric clipping (rect not contained in its clip_rect). This
immediately surfaced a *real* clip that the noise had masked (finding #4).

## Open (reported, not yet fixed)

### 4. Toolbar buttons vertically clipped — `indicators_btn`, `widgets_btn`
Repro: `dev/scenarios/572_design_audit_baseline.json`. With the corrected
`is_clipped`, these two buttons (rect h≈37, top y≈87) overflow the toolbar clip
band (y 73→121) by ~3px at the bottom. Minor but real. Related: the
`dialog.indicator_editor.0` panel stays open across `reset` and `CloseAllDialogs`
(docked/stuck) and overlaps this toolbar region — worth checking whether the
editor panel is squeezing the toolbar row. Severity: low (UI polish).

### 5. Reset after rapid symbol churn leaves a stale symbol
Repro: `dev/scenarios/645_stress_recovery_after_thrash.json`. After thrashing
through several symbols (with indicator adds), then `reset` + `SwapPaneSymbol("SPY")`
+ 1.2 s settle, the pane still shows `AMD` (a thrashed symbol). Suggests an
in-flight async history load from before the reset completes afterward and
clobbers the post-reset symbol — i.e. `reset`/symbol-swap does not cancel or
supersede pending loads. Severity: medium (correctness under rapid input).

## Minor notes (not failing the suite)
- **Rapid timeframe switching lag:** with <~1.2 s between changes, the pane's
  timeframe field lags the requested tf until the async load lands. Arguably
  correct (label follows data), but rapid users see a stale tf briefly.
- **Touch target:** `toolbar.timeframe_picker` is 24px (< 28px min) — 1 contract
  violation in the design audit.
- **Capture gap:** `pane.N.header` and `status_bar.connection` are registered
  without rects (empty_rects in `/design-audit`); harmless but noisy.
