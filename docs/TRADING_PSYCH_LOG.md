but to convert into enforceable rules for the Sentinel discipline layer (see

The point of this file: the lesson sticks in the **system**, not in raw nerve endings.

## Entry — NQ collapse day (NFP)

**The setup (read was correct):** Knew NQ would hit at least 29,800. The 30000 put sat at
**$15 all day** — cheap, aligned with my own confirmed bearish read, would have paid huge.

**What I did:**
- **Refused the $15 put.** Reason: "it was continuation." The market was already falling, so
  taking it felt like getting in *late* — being the sucker. High-probability, matched my read,
  rejected on ego.
- **Took a contrarian long at the 200-day** instead — fading the downtrend, "calling the
  bottom." Felt clever. Low-probability, fought the trend, **lost.**

**The mechanism — THE INVERSION:**
Trade selection is driven by **ego** (which trade makes me feel clever / not a late sucker),
**not by edge** (which trade has the higher probability / matches my read). And ego systematically
selects the *worst* trades:
- Rejects **continuation in the direction of my own confirmed read** (high-prob) because late = sucker.
- Takes **contrarian turn-calls against the trend** (low-prob) because calling the turn = clever / hero entry.

So I refuse the trade that matches my analysis and take the one that contradicts the move.
Perfectionism + fear-of-being-the-sucker = I can only take the "ultimate" entry, never the meat.

**The rules it implies:**
1. Continuation **in the direction of my confirmed read** IS the trade. The meat of the move, not the top.
2. A trade that feels "too late / sucker-ish" is often the tell it's the **right, high-prob** trade.
3. A trade that feels "clever / contrarian / calling the turn" is the tell it's the **ego** trade — gate it.
4. There is no "ultimate entry" — it only exists in hindsight. Demanding it = freezing on every real edge.

**Sentinel spec inputs:**
- Gate / require typed justification for **counter-trend** entries (long while trend+day-type are down).
- Surface the **read-aligned continuation trade** via the forced-visibility plan-card (the "$15 put staring at me")
  so the high-prob trade is unmissable instead of mentally vetoed.
- The "sucker-fear veto" has no chartable basis — treat "it's continuation" as a non-reason, same as "it's too obvious."

**Honest caveat:** the lesson is the *mechanism* (ego picks the worst trades), NOT "I should chase every
continuation." Continuations still need a real setup + a stop + a hold-rule (the seatbelt). The fix is taking
read-aligned trades *with a seatbelt*, not becoming aggressive.
